The dates change the shortlist

Late April into the first days of May is still spring in most of these places. Air can feel pleasant for sitting out, but the sea is cool almost everywhere. For a three-night trip this matters more than summer brochure photos suggest: a 4–4.5 hour flight plus a long transfer eats a lot of a long weekend, and some resort strips are not fully awake yet.

Warmest & most reliable Tenerife, Fuerteventura, south Turkey, Tunisia, Agadir. Days about 22–25°C. Sea still only about 18–20°C.
Pleasant, not swim-hot Fuengirola, Mallorca, Salou, Oropesa, Crete. Days about 20–23°C. Sea about 16–18°C.
Mild and changeable Costa Brava, Corfu, Durrës. Days about 17–21°C, cooler nights, more chance of a shower. Sea about 14–17°C.
Least beach-like Sunny Beach, Bulgaria. Often mid-teens to low 20s, Black Sea still cold. Fine for a cheap city-by-the-sea break, weak as a beach week.

What we scored

Your requirements

  • Health and safety — crime, petty theft, nightlife risk, FCDO travel advice, food and water hygiene.
  • Ease of transport — typical London flight time, how close the resort is to the airport, and whether 20–30 people can move as a group without hiring lots of taxis.
  • Beach quality — sand vs pebbles, cleanliness, space, how usable it is at the start of May.
  • Amenities — supermarket, pharmacy, cash machine, chemist, and a walkable strip for snacks and drinks.
  • Hospital access — nearest proper hospital, and what a UK or EU passport holder actually uses there (GHIC/EHIC vs insurance only).
  • Food quality — beyond the hotel buffet: local restaurants, hygiene, and whether the area is known for decent food or purely cheap tourist plates.
  • Climate, 30 Apr–3 May — daytime heat, evening chill, rain risk, and whether the sea is swimable. May averages are used where early-May data is thin.

Extra criteria added for this group

  • Three-night practicality — long flights and 90-minute transfers waste a short trip.
  • Group fit — can one hotel or two neighbouring hotels take 20–30 people, and is the resort mixed-age rather than a stag strip.
  • Resort character — family package, party town, quiet residential, or purpose-built all-inclusive.
  • Insurance and entry — GHIC/EHIC in the EU, no reciprocal cover in Turkey, Tunisia, Morocco or Albania. UK passports are visa-free for short stays in all of these countries, but Turkey needs 150 days of passport validity.
  • English and cards — how easy it is to sort a pharmacy, taxi or bill if someone is unwell.
  • Season opening — some pools, beach bars and even hotel wings are still closed in early May.

Scores are a committee aid, not science. They are based on typical resort character, official UK health advice, and long-term April/May climate averages — not a live quote for a named hotel.

Committee recommendation

Deduplicated, the Loveholidays list is 26 places, but many are the same coast. Polling all of them will split the vote. Suggested move: send five options to the main group, and park the rest unless someone on the committee has a strong personal reason to revive one.

Poll this

Fuengirola

Best all-round mainland Spain option. Malaga is an easy flight, the transfer is short, the town has real shops and pharmacies, and Costa del Sol hospitals are used to British visitors. GHIC/EHIC works. Weather is a step up from Costa Brava.

Poll this

Costa Brava town cluster

Treat Calella, Malgrat de Mar and Santa Susanna as one choice. Short flights into Girona or Barcelona, walkable strips, hospital at Calella or Blanes, GHIC/EHIC. Trade-off: coolest Spanish sea and changeable spring weather. Prefer these over Lloret if the group is mixed-age.

Poll this

East Mallorca

Treat Cala Millor and Sa Coma as one choice. Proper sandy beaches, lots of 3★ all-inclusive stock, Hospital de Manacor nearby, GHIC/EHIC. Transfer from Palma is the weak point (about 60–80 minutes).

Poll this

Side or Alanya

Best weather-and-value package on the list if people accept no GHIC, pay-as-you-go private hospitals, and a longer flight. Strong all-inclusive culture for a big group that is happy to stay on the hotel grounds. Side is a bit prettier; Alanya is a bigger town with more shops.

Poll this

Crete · Adelianos Kampos

Greek food is the strongest on this list, GHIC/EHIC works, Rethymno hospital is close, and May weather is nicer than Costa Brava. Flight is longer than Spain. Dassia, Corfu is the backup Greece option if you want a livelier strip and a slightly shorter hop.

Park unless weather wins

Canaries

Warmest and sunniest, but a 4.5 hour flight for three nights, and the two named resorts are awkward: Callao Salvaje is quiet and not a classic sandy strip; Nuevo Horizonte is more residential than a group beach resort.

Shortlist

Calella

Costa Brava · Spain · Girona or Barcelona

Proper town, not just a hotel strip. Hospital Sant Jaume is in Calella. Promenade, shops, pharmacy, trains along the coast. Beach is a long urban sandy stretch — decent, not wild. Strongest amenities score on this coast.

Shortlist

Malgrat de Mar

Costa Brava · Spain · next to Santa Susanna

You already noted it is close to the beach — that is fair. Wide sandy beach, package hotels, supermarket and pharmacy in town, Calella hospital a short hop. Very similar to Santa Susanna; pick whichever hotel block can take the group.

Shortlist

Santa Susanna

Costa Brava · Spain · between Malgrat and Pineda

Purpose-built British-friendly strip. Easy for a large group that wants to walk between hotel, beach and a few bars. Slightly less “real town” than Calella or Malgrat. Same hospital and weather story.

Discuss

Lloret de Mar

Costa Brava · Spain · party reputation

Best nightlife and most shops on this coast, Hospital de Blanes nearby. For a mixed 20–30 group it can feel loud, drink-heavy and a bit seedy on the main strip. Only poll this if the committee wants a livelier trip.

Discuss

Tossa de Mar

Costa Brava · Spain · prettier, smaller

Best beach and old-town setting of the Spanish list. Weaker for 20–30 people: fewer big all-inclusive blocks, tighter streets, longer transfer from Girona/Barcelona, and a smaller supermarket scene. Lovely for a couple, fiddly for a coach group.

Shortlist

Fuengirola

Costa del Sol · Malaga airport · about 25–35 minutes

Weather: days 21–23°C, nights 13–15°C, sea 16–18°C. Sunniest of the mainland options. Sea still fresh.

Long sandy urban beach, huge supermarket and pharmacy choice, English widely spoken, public and private hospitals on the Costa del Sol used to UK patients. GHIC/EHIC in public hospitals; hotels may still send you private, so insurance stays essential. Food is ordinary tourist-Spanish but with real Spanish town options behind the front.

Discuss

Salou

Costa Dorada · Reus or Barcelona

Weather: days 19–22°C, nights 10–13°C, sea 16–18°C. A little warmer than Costa Brava.

Good sandy beach and a proper resort strip. Reus can be a handy short flight; Barcelona makes the transfer long. Family-plus-nightlife mix, PortAventura nearby. Hospitals in Reus or Tarragona. Fine if a cheap Reus flight appears, otherwise Fuengirola is cleaner for a short trip.

Discuss

Oropesa del Mar

Costa de Azahar · Castellón or Valencia

Weather: days 20–22°C, nights 11–14°C, sea 16–18°C.

Blue Flag sandy beaches and a quieter Spanish feel. Weaker UK package scene, fewer English-speaking desks, and airport choice is less convenient than Malaga or Girona. Hospital de la Plana is inland at Vila-real. Only keep if a standout hotel deal appears.

Majorca, east and south-east

Same island, same airport, same hospital story. Beach quality is the split.

Poll Cala Millor / Sa Coma
30 Apr–early May: days 19–22°C, nights 11–14°C, sea 16–18°C. Often brighter than Costa Brava. Palma transfer to the east coast is the group tax: typically 60–80 minutes, longer to Cala Ratjada.
Shortlist

Cala Millor

East Majorca · Palma ~70 minutes

Long pale sandy beach, the classic east-coast package resort. Shops, pharmacy and a walkable front. Hospital de Manacor is the local general hospital; Palma for major trauma. Best beach-plus-amenities mix on the island list.

Shortlist

Sa Coma

Next door to Cala Millor

Quieter sister of Cala Millor, still a proper sandy bay, still walkable. Good if the group wants the same beach quality with less strip noise. Treat as interchangeable with Cala Millor when you poll.

Discuss

Cala d'Or

South-east coves · prettier, more spread out

Smaller sandy coves and a nicer harbour-town feel. Food a bit better. Worse for a large group: the resort is spread across several calas, so people get split. Keep only if two hotels sit together.

Discuss

Cala Ratjada

North-east tip · longest transfer

Real harbour town with shops and a mix of beaches. Transfer from Palma is the longest of the island set, which hurts a three-night trip. Otherwise perfectly usable.

Park

Cales de Mallorca

East coast · more isolated hotel pocket

Purpose-built and further from a proper town. Beaches are smaller coves (Cala Domingos and similar). Fine if you never leave the hotel; weak if someone needs a late pharmacy run or the group wants an evening wander.

Callao Salvaje

South-west Tenerife · TFS about 20–30 minutes

Weather: days 23–25°C, nights 16–18°C, sea about 19–20°C, little rain. The most reliable sunshine of the whole list.

Quiet residential / villa area, not Los Cristianos. Playa de Ajabo is a small rebuilt beach, not a wide sandy bay. Shops are limited; Costa Adeje hospitals (Hospiten Sur and similar) are a drive, not a stroll. GHIC/EHIC works in public care. For this group, the climate is excellent and the named resort is a poor match.

Park

Nuevo Horizonte

Fuerteventura · north of the island

Weather: days 23–24°C, windy, sea about 18–19°C. Bright but the Atlantic can feel chilly and choppy.

More a residential urbanización than a group beach resort. Local beach is ordinary; better sand is a drive. Hospital General is in Puerto del Rosario. Same GHIC story as Spain, same long flight problem as Tenerife.

Greek islands

Strong food, EU healthcare, slightly longer flights than Spain.

Poll Crete
Shortlist

Adelianos Kampos

Crete · near Rethymno · Heraklion or Chania

Weather: days 20–23°C, nights 12–14°C, sea 17–18°C. Drier and milder than Corfu in May.

Sandy/shingle resort beach next to a real town (Rethymno) with shops, pharmacies and tavernas. General hospital in Rethymno; GHIC/EHIC in state care. Island hospitals are more limited than mainland Spain, and FCDO notes island ambulance gaps, so insurance still matters. Best food on the list.

Discuss

Dassia

Corfu · about 20 minutes from CFU

Weather: days 19–22°C, nights 12–14°C, sea 16–17°C. Wetter than Crete in spring.

Lively Corfu strip, sand and shingle, bars and shops on the road, Corfu Town hospital not far. Transfer is easier than Crete. Beach is less special and May is greener and damper. Good backup if Crete flights are awkward.

Turkey

Warmest Mediterranean package value. No GHIC. Insurance is not optional.

Poll Side or Alanya
30 Apr–early May: days 21–25°C, nights 12–16°C, sea 18–20°C and rising. Among the best swim chances after the Canaries. Evenings can still need a jumper inland or at Kemer.
Shortlist

Side

Antalya coast · AYT about 60–75 minutes

Sandy bays, a pleasant old town, plenty of all-inclusive hotels used to UK groups. Shops and pharmacies in the resort. Manavgat / Side hospitals and private clinics; GHIC is not valid. British prescriptions are not accepted in Turkish pharmacies. Strongest “warm beach plus group hotel” option.

Shortlist

Alanya

Antalya coast · AYT about 2 hours

Bigger working town: easier to buy food, drinks and medicine than a pure resort. Long sandy/pebble mix beaches. Transfer from Antalya is the longest of the Turkey set and hurts a three-night trip. Same healthcare rules as Side.

Discuss

Kemer

Antalya · pine hills and hotel pockets

Closer to Antalya airport than Alanya. Beaches are more mixed (pebble, imported sand, hotel platforms). Feels more “compound hotel” than town. Fine if the group wants to stay put.

Discuss

Marmaris

Dalaman · harbour town

Proper town amenities and nightlife. Beach in the centre is ordinary; Icmeler nearby is better. Dalaman flight plus a winding transfer. Livelier than Side, slightly weaker as a beach holiday.

Discuss

Ölüdeniz

Dalaman · famous lagoon, hillside resort

Best-looking beach on the whole list. Weak for 20–30 people on a budget: fewer cheap all-inclusive blocks, steep walking, lagoon gets busy, Fethiye hospital is a drive. Beautiful, not practical as the default.

Tunisia and Morocco

Warm, cheap, and a bigger health-and-safety conversation for a large UK group.

Do not poll first
Park

Sousse

Tunisia · Monastir or Enfidha

Weather: days 22–25°C, nights 14–16°C, sea 17–19°C. Warm enough to feel like a holiday.

FCDO advises against travel to parts of Tunisia (not the Sousse resort strip itself, but insurance questions will come up). No free care for visitors — pay on the spot, sometimes before treatment. Sahloul hospital and private clinics exist. Food hygiene needs more care than Spain. A large mixed group will spend more time on risk debate than on the beach.

Park

Djerba

Tunisia · island airport · listed under Turkey on your sheet by mistake

Weather: days 23–26°C, sea 18–20°C. One of the warmer seas on the list.

Better beaches than Sousse, more isolated hotel zone. Same healthcare and FCDO-context issues. Houmt Souk hospital is basic; serious cases mean evacuation planning via insurance.

Park

Agadir

Morocco · AGA · long Atlantic beach

Weather: days 23–24°C, evenings cooler, sea 17–18°C, can be windy or cloudy. Warm air, Atlantic water.

Huge sandy beach and a real city for shops and 24-hour pharmacies. FCDO: public hospitals are limited, private is better, no GHIC, food/water hygiene and allergy labelling are weaker, stray dogs/rabies risk. For 20–30 people this is more admin and more “what if someone is ill” than Spain or Greece.

Albania and Bulgaria

Cheap, but the weakest match to “beach holiday in early May”.

Do not poll first
Park

Durrës

Albania · Tirana airport about 40–50 minutes

Weather: days 19–22°C, sea 16–17°C. Similar to a cool Costa Brava, less resort polish.

Long urban beach of mixed cleanliness. City amenities are actually good. FCDO is blunt: medical facilities are very poor outside Tirana, tap water is not safe, no GHIC. American Hospital has a Durres clinic; serious care still means Tirana or evacuation. Too many health caveats for a first group trip.

Park

Sunny Beach

Bulgaria · Burgas · Black Sea

Weather: days 16–20°C, nights can drop to single figures early on, sea 13–16°C. Least convincing beach weather on the list.

Huge sandy strip and cheap all-inclusive, GHIC/EHIC works in public care, but FCDO says many hospitals are basic and tourist clinics overcharge. Party reputation, variable food, and early May is not swimming weather. Easy to reject for this date.

Healthcare in one page

UK and EU cards work

Spain (including Mallorca and the Canaries), Greece and Bulgaria: a UK GHIC or still-valid EHIC gets medically necessary state care on the same basis as a local. It is not private-hospital cover and it never covers flying someone home. Hotels in tourist areas often call a private clinic first — say you want the public hospital if you are relying on the card.

Emergency number in Spain, Greece, Bulgaria and Turkey: 112.

Insurance only

Turkey, Tunisia, Morocco and Albania have no UK reciprocal cover for ordinary holidaymakers. Tunisia expects payment on the spot. Morocco’s public hospitals are limited. Albania’s FCDO note is the starkest: facilities are very poor outside Tirana, and you should insure for air ambulance.

EU nationals in the group should still carry their own EHIC/GHIC equivalent for Spain and Greece. In Turkey and North Africa they are in the same boat as UK travellers: private insurance.

Suggested next steps for the small committee

  1. Agree whether weather or short flight / easy hospital is the tie-break. That single choice decides Spain vs Turkey vs (maybe) the Canaries.
  2. Send the main group five named choices: Fuengirola, Costa Brava (Calella / Malgrat / Santa Susanna), East Mallorca (Cala Millor / Sa Coma), Side or Alanya, Crete (Adelianos Kampos).
  3. On the poll, ask two extra questions: “Are you happy with a 4+ hour flight for three nights?” and “Any medical needs we must plan around?”
  4. Once a coast wins, pick the hotel by who can actually room 20–30 people on the same board basis, not by the prettiest photo.
  5. Before anyone pays a deposit, re-check FCDO travel advice and make GHIC/insurance a condition of coming.
