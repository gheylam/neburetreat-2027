The dates change the shortlist

Late April into the first days of May is still spring in most of these places. Air can feel pleasant for sitting out, but the sea is cool almost everywhere. For a three-night trip this matters more than summer brochure photos suggest: a 4–4.5 hour flight plus a long transfer eats a lot of a long weekend, and some resort strips are not fully awake yet.

Warmest & most reliable Tenerife, Fuerteventura, south Turkey, Tunisia, Agadir. Days about 22–25°C. Sea still only about 18–20°C.
Pleasant, not swim-hot Fuengirola, Mallorca, Salou, Oropesa, Crete. Days about 20–23°C. Sea about 16–18°C.
Mild and changeable Costa Brava, Corfu, Durrës. Days about 17–21°C, cooler nights, more chance of a shower. Sea about 14–17°C.
Least beach-like Sunny Beach, Bulgaria. Often mid-teens to low 20s, Black Sea still cold. Fine for a cheap city-by-the-sea break, weak as a beach week.

What we scored

Your requirements

  • Health and safety — crime, petty theft, nightlife risk, FCDO travel advice, food and water hygiene.
  • Ease of transport — typical London flight time, how close the resort is to the airport, and whether 20–30 people can move as a group without hiring lots of taxis.
  • Beach quality — sand vs pebbles, cleanliness, space, how usable it is at the start of May.
  • Amenities — supermarket, pharmacy, cash machine, chemist, and a walkable strip for snacks and drinks.
  • Hospital access — nearest proper hospital, and what a UK or EU passport holder actually uses there (GHIC/EHIC vs insurance only).
  • Food quality — beyond the hotel buffet: local restaurants, hygiene, and whether the area is known for decent food or purely cheap tourist plates.
  • Climate, 30 Apr–3 May — daytime heat, evening chill, rain risk, and whether the sea is swimable. May averages are used where early-May data is thin.

Extra criteria added for this group

  • Three-night practicality — long flights and 90-minute transfers waste a short trip.
  • Group fit — can one hotel or two neighbouring hotels take 20–30 people, and is the resort mixed-age rather than a stag strip.
  • Resort character — family package, party town, quiet residential, or purpose-built all-inclusive.
  • Insurance and entry — GHIC/EHIC in the EU, no reciprocal cover in Turkey, Tunisia, Morocco or Albania. UK passports are visa-free for short stays in all of these countries, but Turkey needs 150 days of passport validity.
  • English and cards — how easy it is to sort a pharmacy, taxi or bill if someone is unwell.
  • Season opening — some pools, beach bars and even hotel wings are still closed in early May.

Scores are a committee aid, not science. They are based on typical resort character, official UK health advice, and long-term April/May climate averages — not a live quote for a named hotel.

Committee recommendation

Many of these places sit on the same coast. Polling all of them will split the vote. Send five options to the main group, and park the rest unless someone has a strong reason to revive one.

Fuengirola

Best all-round mainland Spain option. About 2 hours 45 minutes from the UK to Malaga, then 25–35 minutes by road. The town has real shops and pharmacies, and Costa del Sol hospitals are used to British visitors. GHIC/EHIC works. Weather is a step up from Costa Brava.

Poll this

Costa Brava town cluster

Treat Calella, Malgrat de Mar and Santa Susanna as one choice. About 2 hours 15 minutes from the UK; Girona is then 45–60 minutes by road, Barcelona about 1 hour 15 minutes. Walkable strips, hospital at Calella or Blanes, GHIC/EHIC. Trade-off: coolest Spanish sea and changeable spring weather. Prefer these over Lloret if the group is mixed-age.

Poll this

East Mallorca

Treat Cala Millor and Sa Coma as one choice. Proper sandy beaches, lots of 3★ all-inclusive stock, Hospital de Manacor nearby, GHIC/EHIC. About 2 hours 30 minutes from the UK to Palma, then more than an hour by road to the east coast — that extra transfer is the weak point.

Poll this

Side or Alanya

Best weather-and-value package on the list if people accept no GHIC, pay-as-you-go private hospitals, and a longer hop. About 4 hours from the UK to Antalya, then 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes by road to Side, or about 2 hours to Alanya. Strong all-inclusive culture for a big group that is happy to stay on the hotel grounds. Side is a bit prettier; Alanya is a bigger town with more shops.

Poll this

Crete · Adelianos Kampos

Greek food is the strongest on this list, GHIC/EHIC works, Rethymno hospital is close, and May weather is nicer than Costa Brava. About 3 hours 45 minutes from the UK, then about 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes by road. Dassia, Corfu is the backup if you want a livelier strip: about 3 hours 15 minutes flying, then 15–25 minutes by road.

Park unless weather wins

Canaries

Warmest and sunniest, but about 4 hours 30 minutes in the air for three nights. Once you land, Callao Salvaje is only 20–30 minutes from Tenerife South and Nuevo Horizonte is 15–25 minutes from Fuerteventura airport — the resorts themselves are the awkward bit, not the road transfer.

30 Apr–3 May vs 28–31 May

The recommendation above is for 30 Apr–3 May. Moving the trip four weeks later is the single biggest weather upgrade available without changing destination. Typical packages at the end of May are about £50 more per person than the £240–£300 band already agreed.

Budget if you move the dates: expect roughly £290–£350 pp, not £240–£300. Same hotels, same all-inclusive band, just later in the season.

Does the poll list change?

No. Keep the same five: Fuengirola, Costa Brava, East Mallorca, Side or Alanya, and Crete.

Late May does not rescue the parked options. The Canaries, Tunisia, Morocco, Durrës and Sunny Beach were set aside for flight length, hospital cover or resort character, not just a cool sea.

What does change is the weighting

On 30 Apr–3 May, weather is a real reason to lean Fuengirola or Turkey. On 28–31 May, Costa Brava and Mallorca become honest beach holidays, and Turkey’s extra heat is less of a reason to accept no GHIC and a longer flight.

Late May also means more hotels, beach bars and supermarket hours are actually open — useful for 20–30 people.

Wide sandy beach, close to the water, package hotels, supermarket and pharmacy in town, Calella hospital a short hop. Very similar to Santa Susanna; pick whichever hotel block can take the group.

UK flight about 2 hours 15 minutes to Girona or Barcelona.
Girona is then about 45–60 minutes by road to the resort; Barcelona is about 1 hour 15 minutes.

Purpose-built British-friendly strip. Easy for a large group that wants to walk between hotel, beach and a few bars. Slightly less “real town” than Calella or Malgrat. Same hospital and weather story.

Best nightlife and most shops on this coast, Hospital de Blanes nearby. For a mixed 20–30 group it can feel loud, drink-heavy and a bit seedy on the main strip. Only poll this if the committee wants a livelier trip.

UK flight about 2 hours 15 minutes to Girona or Barcelona.

Best beach and old-town setting of the Spanish list. Weaker for 20–30 people: fewer big all-inclusive blocks, tighter streets, longer transfer from Girona/Barcelona, and a smaller supermarket scene. Lovely for a couple, fiddly for a coach group.

Fuengirola

UK flight about 2 hours 45 minutes to Malaga.
Then about 25–35 minutes by road to the resort.

UK flight about 2 hours 15 minutes to Reus or Barcelona.

Weather: days 19–22°C, nights 10–13°C, sea 16–18°C. A little warmer than Costa Brava.

Good sandy beach and a proper resort strip. Reus can be a handy short flight; Barcelona makes the transfer long. Family-plus-nightlife mix, PortAventura nearby. Hospitals in Reus or Tarragona. Fine if a cheap Reus flight appears, otherwise Fuengirola is cleaner for a short trip.

Weather: days 20–22°C, nights 11–14°C, sea 16–18°C.

Blue Flag sandy beaches and a quieter Spanish feel. Weaker UK package scene, fewer English-speaking desks, and airport choice is less convenient than Malaga or Girona. Hospital de la Plana is inland at Vila-real. Only keep if a standout hotel deal appears.

Healthcare in one page

UK and EU cards work

Spain (including Mallorca and the Canaries), Greece and Bulgaria: a UK GHIC or still-valid EHIC gets medically necessary state care on the same basis as a local. It is not private-hospital cover and it never covers flying someone home. Hotels in tourist areas often call a private clinic first — say you want the public hospital if you are relying on the card.

Emergency number in Spain, Greece, Bulgaria and Turkey: 112.

Insurance only

Turkey, Tunisia, Morocco and Albania have no UK reciprocal cover for ordinary holidaymakers. Tunisia expects payment on the spot. Morocco’s public hospitals are limited. Albania’s FCDO note is the starkest: facilities are very poor outside Tirana, and you should insure for air ambulance.

EU nationals in the group should still carry their own EHIC/GHIC equivalent for Spain and Greece. In Turkey and North Africa they are in the same boat as UK travellers: private insurance.

Suggested next steps for the small committee

  1. Agree the dates first: 30 Apr–3 May (cheaper, cooler sea) or 28–31 May (about £50 more pp, much more like a beach holiday). Then agree whether weather or a short flight / easy hospital is the tie-break.
  2. Send the main group the same five named choices on either date: Fuengirola, Costa Brava (Calella / Malgrat / Santa Susanna), East Mallorca (Cala Millor / Sa Coma), Side or Alanya, Crete (Adelianos Kampos).
  3. On the poll, ask two extra questions: “Are you happy with a 4+ hour flight for three nights?” and “Any medical needs we must plan around?”
  4. Once a coast wins, pick the hotel by who can actually room 20–30 people on the same board basis, not by the prettiest photo.
  5. Before anyone pays a deposit, re-check FCDO travel advice and make GHIC/insurance a condition of coming.
