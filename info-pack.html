Where we are going

Fuengirola is a proper town on the Costa del Sol, about 25–35 minutes by road from Malaga airport. The hotel sits on Avenida de las Gaviotas, roughly 100 metres from Playa Las Gaviotas and the promenade. Shops, pharmacies, cash machines and restaurants are a walk, not a taxi hunt. Spain is in the EU, so a UK GHIC or still-valid EHIC works in public hospitals, and English is widely spoken.

Early May here is pleasant sitting-out weather — typically about 21–23°C in the day — but the sea is still cool, around 17–18°C. Evenings need a jumper. Pack for a beach town in spring, not August.

The hotel

Monarque Cendrillón

Avenida de las Gaviotas s/n, 29640 Fuengirola. About 100 metres from Las Gaviotas beach and the promenade. It sits next to Hotel Monarque Fuengirola Park and uses that buffet restaurant. There is a 24-hour reception, an outdoor pool and a children’s pool, a pool bar, and paid paddle / tennis courts. Entertainment is mainly a summer thing, so do not expect a full animation programme in early May.

This is a 3★ town-and-beach hotel, not a remote all-inclusive compound. That is the point: you can walk out for tapas, a pharmacy, or a supermarket if you want something the buffet does not have.

Questions

Why is Lucy’s price different from the Loveholidays website?

The website is a live quote for leftover individual rooms. It moves, and it is not holding 12 rooms together. Lucy is a Loveholidays group coordinator. She has a block for 23 people on these flights, with a monthly payment plan. Group holds and website flash prices are not the same product. Do not book on the website if you want to be in this group — you will sit on different flights or lose the payment plan.

Why Sunday, not the Thursday–Sunday weekend?

The Sunday Gatwick departure and Wednesday Luton return is about £100 cheaper per person than starting at the end of the previous week. That is why we moved. Monday 3 May is a UK bank holiday, so that Monday is already off. You still need Tuesday and Wednesday as annual leave, because we land at Luton at 21:55.

How much annual leave do I need?

Two days: Tuesday 4 May and Wednesday 5 May. Monday 3 May 2027 is the Early May UK bank holiday, so most people already have that day off. We fly on Sunday 2 May and land at Luton at 21:55 on Wednesday, so Wednesday is still a leave day.

Is travel to the UK airports in the price?

No. Lucy’s figures cover the flights, hotel and board, plus the optional Malaga transfer and insurance if you take those. Getting yourself to Gatwick on Sunday and home from Luton on Wednesday night is on you. Do not add it into the £328 / £284 or the £366 / £322 working totals — budget it as a separate extra.

Which board should I pick?

That is the WhatsApp poll. All inclusive is more at the hotel and less thinking. Half board is cheaper and assumes we will eat and drink in town for lunch. There is no recommended default. The whole group will take the option that wins the poll.

Can some people go all inclusive and others half board?

No. Lucy is booking one board basis for the whole block.

What if I cannot take the leave, or I need to drop out?

Tell Hey as soon as you know, and before 2 October if you can. After deposits are paid, Loveholidays’ change and cancellation rules apply — you may not get the money back.

Can I book my own flights and just stay at the hotel?

Not on this quote. The price is a flight-plus-hotel package on those two flights. Separate bookings break the group hold.

What about insurance and hospitals?

Take travel insurance. Costa del Sol hospitals are used to British visitors. A GHIC or EHIC helps with medically necessary state care; hotels often call a private clinic first, so say you want the public hospital if you are relying on the card. Emergency number in Spain is 112. Insurance is what covers repatriation.

Will the sea be warm enough to swim?

For some people, yes; for most, it will feel fresh. Air in early May is the pleasant bit. If you only want a warm sea, this date was never going to deliver that anywhere on a three-night UK package.
