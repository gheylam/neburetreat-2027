Where we are going

Key points:

  • Fuengirola is a town on the Costa del Sol.
  • The drive from Malaga airport to Fuengirola takes about 25 to 35 minutes.
  • The hotel is about 100 meters from the beach and the promenade.
  • Daytime air in early May is about 21 to 23°C.
  • The sea is about 17 to 18°C.
  • A GHIC is a UK card for state care. That card works in public hospitals in Spain.
More detail

Shops, pharmacies, cash machines, and restaurants are in walking distance. You do not need a taxi for those errands. Spain is in the EU. An EHIC is the older health card. A valid EHIC still works in public hospitals. Many people in Fuengirola speak English.

The evening in early May is cool. Bring a sweater. Pack for a beach town in spring.

Ideas for things to do

Key points:

  • The beach is a short walk from the hotel.
  • Early May is good for sitting outside.
  • The sea can still feel cold.
  • The hotel has indoor lounges, outdoor lounges, and an outdoor pool.
  • A large group can walk in town, hike, or visit Mijas old town.
  • You can use a spa, swim, play beach volleyball, or try watersports nearby.
  • The coastal train to Malaga takes about 45 minutes.
  • The Alhambra in Granada is a long day trip.
More detail

Playa Las Gaviotas is the beach by the hotel. Use of the beach depends on the temperature that day. The hotel lounges are indoors and outdoors. The pool at the hotel is outdoors. The hotel also has a pool for children.

Nearby, you can look for a spa or an indoor swim. You can also play beach volleyball. Watersports are available outside the resort. Watersports are optional. Some people in the group do not want watersports.

A large group can hike or walk around the town. Mijas Pueblo is the old town in the hills above Fuengirola. A visit there can take the whole group.

The C1 coastal train runs between Fuengirola and Malaga center. The ride takes about 45 minutes. Trains run often through the day. A group of 23 will not sit together in one carriage.

If you want a day in Malaga, go in small groups. We will not book a coach unless people ask for one. You can stay in Fuengirola for the whole trip.

The Alhambra is a palace in Granada. Granada is a long trip from Fuengirola. A day trip is possible if you want to go. The area around the hotel already has enough to do.

Questions

Why is the price from Lucy different from the Loveholidays website?

The website price is a live quote for rooms and flights that are left. That quote moves, and it does not hold 12 rooms together. When the flight gets more booked up, the price increases. When the number of rooms LoveHolidays has get used up, they need to request more rooms (usually at a higher price.)

In other words, holiday agencies like LoveHolidays has good price for a small group of people but not necessarily for a whole school bus.

Why Sunday, not the Friday to Sunday weekend?

The Sunday Gatwick flight and the Wednesday Luton return cost about £100 less per person. That price gap is why the trip moved. Monday 3 May is a UK bank holiday. The group can still use that day off.

How much annual leave do I need?

You need two days of annual leave. The days are Tuesday 4 May and Wednesday 5 May. Monday 3 May is a UK bank holiday, so that day is already off.

Is travel to the UK airports in the price?

No. The quote from Lucy covers the flights, the hotel, and the meals plan. The optional £22 insurance is separate if you take it. You pay for your own trip to Gatwick on Sunday. You pay for your own trip home from Luton on Wednesday night. Budget that travel as a separate cost.

Which meals plan do I pick?

The WhatsApp poll is where you pick. All inclusive covers more food and drink at the hotel. Half board costs less. On half board you buy lunch and drinks separately. There is no default meals plan. Hey and Lucy can run two bookings if the group splits.

If you share a room, your roommate must pick the same meals plan.

Lucy is the Loveholidays travel agent for this trip. Hey asked Lucy questions for two weeks. The questions were about the group price. The group price is higher than a booking you make alone on the website. Lucy answered those questions and helped the committee.

Can some people go all inclusive and others half board?

Yes. We can make two separate bookings. If you share a room, you and your roommate must pick the same meals plan. All inclusive pairs with all inclusive. Half board pairs with half board.

Why do I pay £19, and an optional £22?

The £19 deposit holds your place with Loveholidays. You then pay the rest in monthly installments into the shared pot. There are eight Fridays from late October to late May. You pay into a separate holiday savings account. Hey will open that account for this trip only. Hey pays Lucy and Loveholidays from that pot. A separate WhatsApp group reports the money each month. The report shows money in, money out, and the balance.

What if I cannot take the leave, or I need to drop out?

Tell Hey as soon as you know. Tell Hey before 2 October if you can. After you pay the deposit, the Loveholidays change rules apply. After you pay the deposit, the Loveholidays cancellation rules apply. Without insurance, you can lose the money.

Can I book my own flights and stay at the hotel?

No. This group booking does not split into a hotel-only place. You could book the flights and at the same hotel yourself separately. You can then stay in the same resort as the group. That choice lets you pick different flights.

What about insurance and hospitals?

Take travel insurance. Hospitals on the Costa del Sol are used to British visitors. A GHIC or an EHIC covers necessary care in a state hospital. Hotels often call a private clinic first. If you use the GHIC, ask for the public hospital. The emergency number in Spain is 112.

Insurance pays for repatriation. Repatriation means a medical flight home.

Will the sea be warm enough to swim?

Some people will swim. Most people will find the sea cold. The pleasant part in early May is the air, not the sea. A warm sea is not what this date offers. A three-night package from the UK at the start of May will not have a warm sea.

Does the hotel offer gluten-free and lactose-free food?

We made sure on Tripadvisor that the restaurant offers gluten-free food. We made sure on Tripadvisor that the restaurant offers lactose-free food. The menu changes every day. The dishes are not the same each day.
