Where we are going

Fuengirola is a proper town on the Costa del Sol, about 25–35 minutes by road from Malaga airport. The hotel sits on Avenida de las Gaviotas, roughly 100 metres from Playa Las Gaviotas and the promenade. Shops, pharmacies, cash machines and restaurants are a walk, not a taxi hunt. Spain is in the EU, so a UK GHIC or still-valid EHIC works in public hospitals, and English is widely spoken.

Early May here is pleasant sitting-out weather — typically about 21–23°C in the day — but the sea is still cool, around 17–18°C. Evenings need a jumper. Pack for a beach town in spring, not August.

The hotel

Monarque Cendrillón

Avenida de las Gaviotas s/n, 29640 Fuengirola. About 100 metres from Las Gaviotas beach and the promenade. It sits next to Hotel Monarque Fuengirola Park and uses that buffet restaurant. There is a 24-hour reception, an outdoor pool and a children’s pool, a pool bar, and paid paddle / tennis courts. Entertainment is mainly a summer thing, so do not expect a full entertainment programme in early May.

This is a 3★ town-and-beach hotel, not a isolated remote all-inclusive compound. Hence you can walk out for tapas, a pharmacy, or a supermarket if you want something the buffet does not have.

How we pay

Everyone pays into one shared holiday pot — a separate savings account Hey will open for this trip only. You pay a small £19 deposit to hold your place, then spread the rest across monthly instalments until May. Prefer to clear your balance in one go? You can still pay the full amount into the same pot.

The pot and the audit WhatsApp — read this

This is the payment system for the whole group. It is not optional bookkeeping in the background — it is how we keep everyone’s money safe and visible.

  1. Separate savings account. Hey will open a dedicated holiday savings account. Every deposit for this trip goes into that account and nowhere else. It is not mixed with personal money.
  2. You pay into the pot. Each month (or in one lump sum if you prefer), you transfer your instalment into that savings account. Hey then pays Loveholidays / Lucy from the pot on the group’s behalf.
  3. Separate WhatsApp audit group. There will be a second WhatsApp chat just for money. Every month Hey will post what came in, what went out, and the running balance, so anyone can check the numbers. That chat is for transparent reporting and audit — not general trip chat.

The pot may pick up a little interest while the money sits there — that stays with the group pot, not anyone’s pocket.

Questions

Why is Lucy’s price different from the Loveholidays website?

The website is a live quote for leftover individual rooms. It moves, and it is not holding 12 rooms together. Lucy is a Loveholidays group coordinator. She has a block for 23 people on these flights, with a monthly payment plan. Group holds and website flash prices are not the same product. Essentially, LoveHoliday has good deals for groups of 6 or 8 but not for a whole minibus.

Why Sunday, not the Friday-Sunday weekend?

The Sunday Gatwick departure and Wednesday Luton return is about £100 cheaper per person than starting at the end of the previous week. That is why we moved. Monday 3 May is a UK bank holiday, so we can still take advantage of that.

How much annual leave do I need?

Two days: Tuesday 4 May and Wednesday 5 May.

Is travel to the UK airports in the price?

No. Lucy’s figures cover the flights, hotel and board, plus the optional insurance if you take those. Getting yourself to Gatwick on Sunday and home from Luton on Wednesday night is on you. We will need to budget it as a separate extra.

Which board should I pick?

That is the WhatsApp poll. All inclusive is more at the hotel and less thinking. Half board is cheaper and assumes you will eat and drink in town for lunch. There is no recommended default. The whole group can split up by making two separate bookings managed by Hey and Lucy.

Lucy is the travel agent from LoveHolidays that Hey has been harrassing for the last two weeks about details and questions. Especially about why on earth is the group booking more expensive than when I book online myself. Lucy has been a very helpful queen throughout the whole process.

Can some people go all inclusive and others half board?

Yes! We can make two separate bookings but the quote should still be the same (assuming the prices hasn't fluctuated when we confirm with lucy).

Why is it £19 + optional £22 to confirm my attendance?

Loveholidays protocol: £19 confirms / holds your place, then the rest is paid in equal monthly instalments into the shared pot (eight Fridays from late October to late May — see How we pay). The optional £22 is Loveholidays’ travel insurance if you want it. Recommended.

Where does my money go, and how do I know it’s safe?

Everyone deposits into a separate holiday savings account Hey will open for this trip only. Hey pays Lucy / Loveholidays from that pot. A separate WhatsApp group is used every month to report and audit money in, money out, and the running balance — so the numbers stay transparent to the whole group. That chat is for money only, not general trip chat.

What if I cannot take the leave, or I need to drop out?

Tell Hey as soon as you know, and before 2 October if you can. After deposits are paid, Loveholidays’ change and cancellation rules apply — you may not get the money back without the insurance.

Can I book my own flights and just stay at the hotel?

Unfortunately no. You maybe able to book the flight and hotel separately yourself and end up in the same location with the rest of us. This could give you a greater level of flexibility on choosing your flights. However our cost takes into consideration of using LoveHoliday's bulk rooms that they have at a more competitive price from the resort compared to you going to the resort directly.

What about insurance and hospitals?

Take travel insurance. Costa del Sol hospitals are used to British visitors. A GHIC or EHIC helps with medically necessary state care; hotels often call a private clinic first, so say you want the public hospital if you are relying on the card. Emergency number in Spain is 112. Insurance is what covers repatriation.

Will the sea be warm enough to swim?

For some people, yes; for most, it will feel fresh. Air in early May is the pleasant bit. If you only want a warm sea, this date was never going to deliver that anywhere on a three-night UK package.
